# Cobalt Holdings: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Cobalt Holdings (COBA) makes form-fill-seal machines, carton erectors and labelling lines for dairy cooperatives, drug packers and cosmetics brands. The company is based in Toronto and works in the packaging machinery industry.

Revenue reached USD 812.8 million in 2025, 8% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 32.1%, among the best in our industry.

Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none. We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 329.7 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Irene Ruiz, Chief Executive
