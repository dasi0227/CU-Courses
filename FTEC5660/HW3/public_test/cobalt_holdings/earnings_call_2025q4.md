# Cobalt Holdings: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Irene Ruiz (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 192.6 million. Demand from cosmetics brand customers stayed healthy.

Olivia Brennan (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Irene Ruiz: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
