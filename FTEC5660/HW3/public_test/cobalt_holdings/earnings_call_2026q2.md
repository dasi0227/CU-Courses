# Cobalt Holdings: earnings call, second quarter 2026

Date: 2026-07-29

Irene Ruiz (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 227.7 million. Demand from cosmetics brand customers stayed healthy.

Olivia Brennan (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Irene Ruiz: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
