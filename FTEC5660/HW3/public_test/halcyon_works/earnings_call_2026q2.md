# Halcyon Works: earnings call, second quarter 2026

Date: 2026-07-29

Maya Moreau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 103.1 million. Demand from electronics assembler customers stayed healthy.

Rachel Patel (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Maya Moreau: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
