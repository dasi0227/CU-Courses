# Halcyon Works: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Halcyon Works (HALC) makes high-purity solvents, coating resins and electrolyte additives for paint makers, battery makers and electronics assemblers. The company is based in Hong Kong and works in the specialty chemicals industry.

Revenue reached USD 345.0 million in 2025, 8% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 32.5%, among the best in our industry.

We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team. Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 202.7 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Maya Moreau, Chief Executive
