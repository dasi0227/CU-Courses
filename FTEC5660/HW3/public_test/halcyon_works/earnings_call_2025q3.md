# Halcyon Works: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Maya Moreau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 94.7 million. Demand from electronics assembler customers stayed healthy.

Rachel Patel (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Maya Moreau: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
