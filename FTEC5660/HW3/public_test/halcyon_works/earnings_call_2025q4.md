# Halcyon Works: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Maya Moreau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 84.6 million. Demand from electronics assembler customers stayed healthy.

Rachel Patel (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Maya Moreau: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
