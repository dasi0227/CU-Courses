# Lindale Holdings: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Nathan Leung (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 99.3 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Henry Sato (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Nathan Leung: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
