# Lindale Holdings: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Lindale Holdings (LIND) makes sweetener blends, plant proteins and flavour emulsions for beverage brands, dairy processors and snack makers. The company is based in Seoul and works in the food ingredients industry.

Revenue reached USD 349.6 million in 2025, 14% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 28.2%, among the best in our industry.

We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team. Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 65.6 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Nathan Leung, Chief Executive
