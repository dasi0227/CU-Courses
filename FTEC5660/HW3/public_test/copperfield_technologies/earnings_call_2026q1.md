# Copperfield Technologies: earnings call, first quarter 2026

Date: 2026-04-28

Leon Brennan (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 249.8 million. Demand from memory-chip maker customers stayed healthy.

Victor Sato (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Leon Brennan: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
