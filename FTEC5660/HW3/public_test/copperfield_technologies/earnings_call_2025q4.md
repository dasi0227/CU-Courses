# Copperfield Technologies: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Leon Brennan (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 213.8 million. Demand from foundry customers stayed healthy.

Victor Sato (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: How do you think about research spending?

Leon Brennan: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
