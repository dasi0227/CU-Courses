# Copperfield Technologies: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Copperfield Technologies (COPP) makes inspection systems, etch modules and metrology tools for foundries, memory-chip makers and packaging houses. The company is based in Taipei and works in the semiconductor equipment industry.

Revenue reached USD 912.1 million in 2025, 28% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 47.0%, among the best in our industry.

We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team. Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 392.9 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Leon Brennan, Chief Executive
