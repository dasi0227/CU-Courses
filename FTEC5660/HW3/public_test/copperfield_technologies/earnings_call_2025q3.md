# Copperfield Technologies: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Leon Brennan (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 241.3 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Victor Sato (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Leon Brennan: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
