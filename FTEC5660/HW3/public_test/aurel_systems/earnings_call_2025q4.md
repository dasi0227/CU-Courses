# Aurel Systems: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Tessa Novak (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 64.0 million. Demand from cosmetics brand customers stayed healthy.

Victor Ng (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Tessa Novak: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
