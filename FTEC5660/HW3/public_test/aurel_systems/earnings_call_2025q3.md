# Aurel Systems: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Tessa Novak (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 72.1 million. Demand from drug packer customers stayed healthy.

Victor Ng (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Tessa Novak: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
