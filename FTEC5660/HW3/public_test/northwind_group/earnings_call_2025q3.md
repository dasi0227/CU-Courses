# Northwind Group: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Mei Moreau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 150.2 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Samuel Ruiz (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns rose sharply this quarter because of a faulty batch of metrology tool parts. We are replacing every affected unit at our own cost.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Mei Moreau: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
