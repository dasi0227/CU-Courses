# Northwind Group: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Mei Moreau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 137.2 million. Demand from packaging house customers stayed healthy.

Samuel Ruiz (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Mei Moreau: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
