# Northwind Group: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Northwind Group (NORT) makes inspection systems, etch modules and metrology tools for foundries, memory-chip makers and packaging houses. The company is based in Taipei and works in the semiconductor equipment industry.

Revenue reached USD 537.0 million in 2025, 25% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 48.8%, among the best in our industry.

Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none. We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 240.4 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Mei Moreau, Chief Executive
