# Granite Group: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Elena Brennan (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 98.3 million. Demand from clinic group customers stayed healthy.

Tessa Kowalski (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Elena Brennan: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
