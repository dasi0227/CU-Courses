# Fernhill Group: earnings call, second quarter 2026

Date: 2026-07-29

Wendy Lau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 180.9 million. Demand from dairy processor customers stayed healthy.

Priya Chen (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Wendy Lau: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
