# Fernhill Group: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Wendy Lau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 168.5 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Priya Chen (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Wendy Lau: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
