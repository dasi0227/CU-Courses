# Fernhill Group: earnings call, first quarter 2026

Date: 2026-04-28

Wendy Lau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 165.3 million. Demand from snack maker customers stayed healthy.

Priya Chen (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: How do you think about research spending?
