# Fernhill Group: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Wendy Lau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 148.8 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Wendy Lau: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
