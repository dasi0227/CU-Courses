# Emberly Systems: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Tomas Kim (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 67.4 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Samuel Lindqvist (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns rose sharply this quarter because of a faulty batch of lidar lens parts. We are replacing every affected unit at our own cost.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Tomas Kim: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
