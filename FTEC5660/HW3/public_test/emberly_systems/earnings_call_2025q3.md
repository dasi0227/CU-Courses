# Emberly Systems: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Tomas Kim (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 72.2 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Samuel Lindqvist (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: How do you think about research spending?

Tomas Kim: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
