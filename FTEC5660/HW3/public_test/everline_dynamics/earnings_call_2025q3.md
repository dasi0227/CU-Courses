# Everline Dynamics: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Elena Lau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 103.7 million. Demand from drug packer customers stayed healthy.

Karen Ruiz (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: How do you think about research spending?

Elena Lau: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
