# Everline Dynamics: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Elena Lau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 94.3 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Karen Ruiz (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns rose sharply this quarter because of a faulty batch of labelling line parts. We are replacing every affected unit at our own cost.

Analyst: How do you think about research spending?

Elena Lau: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
