# Elmstead Technologies: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Tessa Brennan (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 100.9 million. Demand from e-commerce warehouse customers stayed healthy.

Nathan Moreau (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Tessa Brennan: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
