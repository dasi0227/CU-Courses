# Ashgrove Labs: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Ashgrove Labs (ASHG) makes robot arms, mobile robots and picking cells for car-parts plants, e-commerce warehouses and electronics factories. The company is based in Singapore and works in the industrial robotics industry.

Revenue reached USD 955.8 million in 2025, 25% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 41.9%, among the best in our industry.

Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none. We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 439.2 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Samuel Hartmann, Chief Executive
