# Ashgrove Labs: earnings call, first quarter 2026

Date: 2026-04-28

Samuel Hartmann (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 257.6 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Grace Novak (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns rose sharply this quarter because of a faulty batch of welding robot parts. We are replacing every affected unit at our own cost.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Samuel Hartmann: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
