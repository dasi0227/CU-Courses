# Hollins Group: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Hollins Group (HOLL) makes infusion pumps, surgical staplers and glucose monitors for hospital networks, clinic groups and buying consortiums. The company is based in Austin and works in the medical devices industry.

Revenue reached USD 522.0 million in 2025, 19% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 59.4%, among the best in our industry.

We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team. Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 238.0 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Irene Kim, Chief Executive
