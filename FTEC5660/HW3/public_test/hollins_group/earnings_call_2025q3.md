# Hollins Group: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Irene Kim (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 133.5 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Omar Patel (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Irene Kim: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
