# Hollins Group: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Irene Kim (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 121.8 million. Demand from hospital network customers stayed healthy.

Omar Patel (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: How do you think about research spending?

Irene Kim: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
