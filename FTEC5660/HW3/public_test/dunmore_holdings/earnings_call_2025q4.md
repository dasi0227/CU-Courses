# Dunmore Holdings: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Daniel Lau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 222.9 million. Demand from automation vendor customers stayed healthy.

Hana Lau (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Daniel Lau: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
