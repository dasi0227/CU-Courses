# Dunmore Holdings: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Daniel Lau (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 245.7 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Hana Lau (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns rose sharply this quarter because of a faulty batch of camera module parts. We are replacing every affected unit at our own cost.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Daniel Lau: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
