# Dunmore Holdings: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Dunmore Holdings (DUNM) makes lidar lenses, camera modules and laser collimators for carmakers, drone makers and automation vendors. The company is based in Toronto and works in the precision optics industry.

Revenue reached USD 950.6 million in 2025, 23% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 43.4%, among the best in our industry.

Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none. We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 443.6 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Daniel Lau, Chief Executive
