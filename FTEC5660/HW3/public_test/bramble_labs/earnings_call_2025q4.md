# Bramble Labs: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Ivan Patel (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 70.5 million. Demand from water utility customers stayed healthy.

Samuel Lau (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Ivan Patel: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
