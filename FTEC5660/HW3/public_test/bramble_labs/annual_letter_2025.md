# Bramble Labs: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Bramble Labs (BRAM) makes membrane filters, UV disinfection units and water softeners for water utilities, beverage plants and chemical plants. The company is based in Lyon and works in the water treatment equipment industry.

Revenue reached USD 279.0 million in 2025, 21% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 37.7%, among the best in our industry.

Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none. We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 231.8 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Ivan Patel, Chief Executive
