# Driftwood Systems: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Driftwood Systems (DRIF) makes high-purity solvents, coating resins and electrolyte additives for paint makers, battery makers and electronics assemblers. The company is based in Toronto and works in the specialty chemicals industry.

Revenue reached USD 604.5 million in 2025, 9% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 34.7%, among the best in our industry.

Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none. We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 276.1 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Omar Kowalski, Chief Executive
