# Driftwood Systems: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Omar Kowalski (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 160.4 million. Demand from battery maker customers stayed healthy.

Nathan Leung (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Omar Kowalski: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
