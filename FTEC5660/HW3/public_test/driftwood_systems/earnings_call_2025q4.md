# Driftwood Systems: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Omar Kowalski (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 147.0 million. Demand from electronics assembler customers stayed healthy.

Nathan Leung (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Omar Kowalski: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
