# Juniper Industries: earnings call, fourth quarter 2025

Date: 2026-02-25

Alicia Novak (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 116.6 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Tomas Wong (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Alicia Novak: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
