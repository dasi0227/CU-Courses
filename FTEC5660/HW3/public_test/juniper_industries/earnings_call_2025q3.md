# Juniper Industries: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Alicia Novak (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 127.9 million. Order books are in line with our plan.

Tomas Wong (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Returns stayed low and product quality was steady.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Alicia Novak: Research is the engine of this company and we fund it accordingly.
