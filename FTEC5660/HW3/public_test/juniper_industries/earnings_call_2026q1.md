# Juniper Industries: earnings call, first quarter 2026

Date: 2026-04-28

Alicia Novak (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 126.9 million. Demand from car-parts plant customers stayed healthy.

Tomas Wong (Chief Financial Officer): Freight costs eased a little. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: Will you keep investing in new products?

Alicia Novak: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
