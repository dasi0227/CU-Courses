# Juniper Industries: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Juniper Industries (JUNI) makes robot arms, mobile robots and picking cells for car-parts plants, e-commerce warehouses and electronics factories. The company is based in Taipei and works in the industrial robotics industry.

Revenue reached USD 494.1 million in 2025, 15% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 40.8%, among the best in our industry.

We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team. Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 373.4 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Alicia Novak, Chief Executive
