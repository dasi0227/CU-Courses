# Falcrest Technologies: earnings call, third quarter 2025

Date: 2025-10-29

Rachel Brennan (Chief Executive): Revenue for the quarter was USD 69.9 million. Demand from car-parts plant customers stayed healthy.

Leon Fischer (Chief Financial Officer): We kept a close eye on costs across our plants. Product quality remained excellent and returns were negligible.

Analyst: How do you think about research spending?

Rachel Brennan: We invest for the long term and our research budget reflects that.
