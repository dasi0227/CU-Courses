# Falcrest Technologies: letter to shareholders, 2025

Date: 2026-03-12

Falcrest Technologies (FALC) makes robot arms, mobile robots and picking cells for car-parts plants, e-commerce warehouses and electronics factories. The company is based in Seoul and works in the industrial robotics industry.

Revenue reached USD 267.5 million in 2025, 27% more than in 2023. Our gross margin was 41.7%, among the best in our industry.

We have a deep bench of leaders who work together as one team. Our people are our greatest strength, and our sales team is second to none.

We have never cut research spending to make a quarter look better.

We plan to invest USD 58.5 million over 2026 to 2028 and will fund it without issuing new shares.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Rachel Brennan, Chief Executive
