## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00006] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not treat numerically equivalent answers as incorrect due to minor formatting differences (e.g., 27.50 vs 27.5). Focus on numerical equivalence rather than exact string matching when evaluating financial calculations.
[err-00035] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not misinterpret ambiguous financial terminology. Phrases like 'decreased market cap' may refer to the new value after decrease rather than the amount of change. Always consider standard financial definitions and context clues to determine the intended meaning. When a phrase could have multiple interpretations, prioritize the interpretation that uses a direct calculation (e.g., shares × price) over one requiring a derived value (e.g., difference between two values).
[err-00044] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not dismiss given market prices in put-call parity problems. When a specific option price (e.g., call price of $25) is provided and the question asks for the 'price difference when the call option is priced at $25,' you must use the given price to calculate the actual numerical difference or the other option's price. The theoretical relationship C - P = S - PV(X) gives the difference, but the specific price allows calculation of the actual values.
[err-00046] helpful=0 harmful=1 :: Do not round intermediate values in multi-step financial calculations, especially for compound interest and other exponentiation problems. Always maintain full precision throughout all calculation steps and only apply rounding to the final answer. Premature rounding, even to 6 decimal places, can introduce errors that affect monetary amounts when rounded to the nearest cent.
[err-00054] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not ignore the economic intuition check for Interest Rate Parity. The currency with the higher nominal interest rate should trade at a forward DISCOUNT relative to the currency with the lower interest rate. If your calculation shows F > S when the foreign/quote currency has a higher interest rate, re-examine your formula application and currency identification. Use this principle as a sanity check.
